  and beverage (Revelstoke Current, undated) versus over 200 winter staff
  (Working Holiday Club, a recruitment listing that may only count roles
  it places). CW-4 in PROMPTS.md is designed to find the Revelstoke
  Current article's publication date and any other public statement of

  is, this step cannot pick a workforce figure and must carry both.

  figure together imply 480 beds, which does not match "over 400" exactly.
  Whether C012's plan changed by the time C011's building opened (both

  dates) needs to be checked before subtracting resort-provided beds from
  workforce housing demand.

  should not be used as a stand-in for RMR's own employee count.
